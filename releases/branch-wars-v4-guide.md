# Branch Wars V4 — playtest quick-start

This is a guide to the **v4.0.0-rc3 playtest (corrected checkpoint68b)**, not the finished comprehensive manual. The full [current player guide](../game/docs/player-guide.md) provides more detail.

## Start safely

1. Export your current campaign and keep your old game folder.
2. Extract `branch-wars-v4.zip`. Keep its six files together.
3. Open `OPEN_BRANCH_WARS.bat`, or the HTML file directly.
4. Start a fresh **Expanded** campaign to test the complete integrated rules. Choose Core for the original experience. Imported campaigns retain their original rules.

V4 is the package label. New Core campaigns use save format8.19 and new Expanded campaigns use9.33; do not edit a save's version number. Older supported campaigns and rematches keep their saved rules and do not acquire new economics or ending rules automatically.

New Expanded campaigns no longer stop because of a score lead or automatic bank
control. Receivership and unresolved funding-covenant failures still end play.
Company ownership and acquisitions remain separate funded decisions. Both peers
must support the new rules; do not mix this build with rc1/rc2.

## Important banking distinctions

Bank forecast now separates recorded loan interest, service fees and recurring
costs, with actual/standing-plan/draft comparisons and income history. Expand
the funding-movement review to distinguish organic deposits from recorded
competition. It is a component view, not a complete change-in-cash statement:
missing history means unavailable, and forecasts cannot know the rival's plan.

Ordinary lending balance is still being tested. More loan volume is not a
guaranteed remedy for losses: servicing uses staff, principal repayments shrink
the portfolio, funding costs consume margin and development spending uses
capital. Review existing workload before assigning everyone to new business.

Before building, review upfront cash/capital, recurring payroll/upkeep and loan
replacement separately. Capacity is not guaranteed sales. A cautious mixed-bank
policy reached120 months in the recorded Balanced trial; two paid-expansion
policies failed at23/32. This is a reason to inspect payback, not a guarantee
that a particular staffing split will work in every campaign.

- Deposits are funding liabilities, not profit. Loan advances exchange cash for a loan asset; they are not earnings.
- Acquiring a company, banking it and lending to it are different relationships. A business does not automatically need or qualify for every product.
- Staff and specialist time are finite. Existing servicing, projects and new business compete for capacity.
- A building is not a license, a staffed service or a free customer book. New capacity still needs funding, qualified people and demand.
- Investment customer assets are not spendable bank or subsidiary cash. Insurance agency commissions come from third-party carriers; underwriting is not included.
- Forecasts are conditional estimates. Rival decisions and future events may change the settled result.

## Multiplayer checklist

Use the identical V4 ZIP on both computers. Review names and distinct colors in the lobby. Host settings changes clear both readiness confirmations. If the other build cannot support the rules, update it rather than changing save metadata.

For LAN, keep the host server running and use its displayed address. For GitHub rooms, keep credentials private and use the intended shared repository. For direct links, both browsers must remain open. Export before a long break, and use the existing recovery controls rather than creating a second copy of the same active session.

## What to test and report

Try simultaneous construction in two markets, staff reallocation, a real company relationship followed by a loan offer, and a multiplayer save/rejoin. Include the package name, campaign month, game mode, action taken and expected/actual result. Export a reproduction only through a private channel; never include access tokens.

For the two-computer GitHub check: use different colors, apply a lobby settings
change and check readiness resets, then complete two months. Export a backup.
Lock only the host's next plan, disconnect and reconnect the guest, then finish
the month exactly once. Export again; reload/resume the host and check both
banks retain their identities and month. A successful connection alone is not
recovery acceptance.

Read [V4 playtest notes](v4-playtest-notes.md) for the verification limits. The old V3 PDF remains a historical manual, not a claim that it covers V4.
