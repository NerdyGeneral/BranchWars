# Unified blueprint and expansion roadmap

This document defines **scope, order and retained decisions**. Use the [implementation ledger](v3-usability.md#master-requirement-inventory-and-finish-gates) for current completion status and [release status](release-status.md) for candidate evidence. Do not maintain a second completed/remaining table here.

## Approved direction

Complete the [Integrated Expanded Edition master objective](expanded-edition-goal.md): open-ended regional banking rivalry, optional Financial Group diversification, meaningful shared resources and modern object-centered controls.

New campaigns offer Core or integrated Expanded. Supported historical modular campaigns retain their saved rules. Campaign rules stay fixed after starting; operating choices can change through explicit decisions and paid implementation. Preserve offline/portable, solo, hotseat, LAN, direct-link and GitHub-room play.

## Retained decisions and boundaries

- D09: separately funded subsidiaries with optional capped parent support.
- D10: deepen existing facilities before extending the catalog; services can share buildings without sharing legal permissions.
- D11: simultaneous lending before broader products; preserve persistent obligations and finite funding.
- D12: six investable operating companies; ownership and banking relationships stay distinct.
- D13: simultaneous share auctions and deliberate takeover mechanics; no free votes, contracts or assets.
- Provisional numerical values may be tuned with documented evidence. They are fictional game safeguards, not statutory limits.
- Managers may propose bounded operating changes; never independently submit turns, close facilities, acquire companies or take major loans.
- Preserve useful subject grouping. Actions belong to their market, office, opportunity, product, department or subsidiary—not navigation bands by cadence.

**Deferred / separately reserved:** National Empire geography, insurance underwriting, unrelated expansions, unrestricted feature independence/presets, additional human seats, circular ownership/derivatives and player-bank stock trading. Do not remove approved campaign options. Remote push/publication/merges/deletion need separate explicit approval.
