## Approved future content

- Facilities: deepen existing offices; then ATM/micro, retail, commercial, wealth,
  digital/advisory, financial centers/hubs. Reconciled costs, staffing, capacity,
  condition, renovations, conversions and local synergies.
- Departments: persistent budgets, objectives, specialists and leaders; bounded
  delegation without autonomous major borrowing, closures, M&A or turn submission.
- Banking products: consumer/deposit/business/mortgage/commercial/CRE/treasury,
  persistent cohorts, deterioration and delayed aggressive-lending consequences.
- Group: separately funded agency/brokerage/wealth businesses with staffing,
  expenses, relationships and capped support. Carriers bear underwriting risk.
- Research: implementation choices with build/buy/outsource/partner, adoption,
  maintenance, costs and risks.
- Marketing/wallet: segment/channel/offer/budget/duration and separable product
  relationships using the same finite underlying customers/books.
- Companies: ownership distinct from banking mandates; simultaneous auctions,
  takeover, bounded liquidity/financing and integration.
