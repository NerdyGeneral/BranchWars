## Working implementation choices

Advertising presets retain $15k/$40k/$80k plus a custom limit of $250k. Community
is a one-month $15k package. The fictional Burs offer is six monthly $30k
payments ($180k total), with cancellation capped at two remaining payments.
These are game balance parameters. Funded scenario tests verify their mechanics;
human playtesting is still needed to assess their balance.

The digital platform has a bank-wide build route ($220k, three months, two
delivery-capacity units) or partner route ($90k, one month, one capacity unit,
$12k monthly vendor cost). These are explicit implementation costs, not free
research effects. Physical studios provide their actual local staffed service.

Shares refer to the holding company, which owns the regulated bank and optional
subsidiaries. Selling bank securities is a separate Treasury action. The first
share-market rules preserve founder control, cap issuance/trading, use funded
outside investors, and require an explicit human order to buy or sell rival
holdings. No client custody money or protected bank capital funds parent orders.
