## Limits and edition selection

To opt into Core 8.20, click Core edition and confirm, even if selected; check Research for six capabilities including RISK & CAPITAL. Untouched setup remains 8.19; confirmed Expanded starts 9.33. Imports/rematches retain saved rules. A model already stripped by the old loader requires an earlier intact export to recover it. Export saves before switching folders or browser origins, retain the previous package, and use matching versions for multiplayer.

Paid model reachability and the census do not establish strategy strength or optimality. Expanded recovery is reachable and changes valid plans; sustained recovery and conventional-lending balance remain unproven. The maintained release-balance configuration uses legacy rules and must not be called current-edition balance. The package smoke uses the bare rematch signature; Claude's separate probe covers the UI's covered-workforce argument. Browser rendering, human play and physical two-computer acceptance remain open. Full Expanded blueprint completion is outside this prerelease.
