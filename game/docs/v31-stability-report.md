## Candidate changes and provisional values

### Paid origination work

Group7 may replace at most two existing additional lending quarter-work units
with the same function's finite paid vendor work. The guard retains every task's
existing coverage, paid teaching, retained service, funding, execution and capital
protections. Total vendor spending for the proposal is capped at the lesser of
$50,000 or 5% of quoted free cash. No headcount or borrower is created by a quota.

The first staffing-only balanced24 run passed technical checks but did not improve
that sample's loan balances. The change is not accepted as a sufficient solution.

### Funded corporate circulation

Group7 has company-world4 and outside-provider books2. At month end, the existing
corporate creditor plus the carrier, agency supplier, facility supplier,
department-leadership supplier and department-function service network each spend
floor(actual cash/50) on local purchases. This provisional 2% rule retains 98% with
each payer; only cash, never receivables, custody assets or future income, funds it.

Both books post named `regional.circulation` entries. Receipts enter the existing
outside customer/supplier pool for next month's funded company sales. This is
not a second bank expense, a grant to a company, a customer/deposit creation or
insurance underwriting. Creditor spending stays inside the existing corporate
cash boundary; external provider spending reconciles with separate private paid
counters and the company's aggregate received counter. Duplicate monthly posting,
wrong source identity, forged totals and mismatched campaign books are rejected.

Pure six-company tests ran 480 months with/without circulation for four demand
scenarios (3,840 domain-months). These are NOT the four complete game economies.

### Recruitment and morale visibility

Group7 AI reserves ordinary recruiting cost before research and new projects,
rather than attempting hiring only with their leftovers. Low morale alone is no
longer an AI prohibition (human hiring never had that prohibition). Up to two hires
below 12 staff, then one below 22, remain provisional limits, not a free target.

No recruitment is proposed with emergency debt, funding excess or severe capital
standing. Current monthly bank profit must cover twice the additional $18,000
gross monthly base payroll per hire. After recruiting, cash must cover $600,000
plus six months of that extra payroll; 10% risk-capital coverage and $200,000 plus
the same payroll reserve must remain. Final shared spending checks still apply.
Actual recruitment pays the ordinary price and activates next month. Later
specialist selection and full-plan affordability can still reduce the proposal.

The actual month120 banks can each fund at least one hire under this proposal.
Tests reject exhausted committed cash, losses, borrowing, insufficient capital
and already-sufficient headcount. This proves the heuristic trap is removable,
not that long-run staffing or profitability is now satisfactory.

Workforce UI now shows workload-only morale change from the shared engine formula,
with Retail/Operations penalties and explicit exclusions. This changes no morale
rule or value. Company UI explains funded aggregate circulation; private payer
books are not exposed.
