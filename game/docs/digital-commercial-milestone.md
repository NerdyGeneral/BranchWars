## Monthly order

1. Validate both complete plans against opening resources. Quotes and staging
   do not change balances or reserve money in another player's private plan.
2. Settle existing company/holding orders; prepare department and office work.
   Apply service policies and deliver funded central work.
3. Start eligible platform projects against **opening learned knowledge**.
4. Operate the existing book and post company service payments/receivables.
   An active empty platform incurs upkeep; staffing and vendors also cost money.
5. Resolve uncertain customer contests. Newly awarded contracts start earning
   next month. Platform completions occur after these contests.
6. Pay research from available cash and finish learned nodes. Dependent research
   and new deployment instructions become eligible in the next planning month.
7. Finish existing group/circulation stages. Accrue and repay outside advances;
   then fund new advances from the lender's remaining actual cash.
8. Complete treasury reinvestment, valuation, records and next-month rate changes.

Research, deployment and winning a customer are distinct. Advertising and
research do not ensure acceptance, full collection or profit. Shared payroll
is bank-wide; the service inspector separates supported direct delivery,
contractor and platform costs from fees and actual cash collected. Deposits
are obligations, never service revenue. The retained-earnings bridge includes
financing-stage interest separately from the operating-profit subtotal.

## Outside-bank funding foundation

**Cedar Reserve Bank** uses stable identity `company:acquisition-lender` and
the existing `companyControlMarket.lender` book. There is no duplicate bank
balance. Its opening equity was funded by the existing outside shareholder,
whose cash was transferred from the corporate outside economy. Its existing
acquisition-finance business and new advances share the same lender cash and
asset ledger. The outside shareholder retains its original investment basis.

Treasury offers one one-month advance per player, $1,000–$250,000 in $1,000
steps. The fixed annual rate is the current Fed upper bound plus 100 basis
points. Monthly interest is principal times annual basis points / 120,000,
rounded up to whole dollars. New requests require at least 8% capital and no
unpaid obligations. Actual funding happens at month end, after all other
spending; it cannot fund other instructions in that same plan.

The lender exchanges cash for a funding claim. The bank records cash and a
matched funding payable; principal does not enter earnings or deposits.
At the following month end interest increases lender claims/equity and reduces
borrower equity through an accrued payable. Repayment uses actual bank cash
above 2% of deposits and other unpaid bills. Payments cover interest first,
then principal. Remaining obligations persist. Each missed month adds five
compliance points and removes three reputation points; subsequent principal
interest adds 200 annual basis points. No automatic rollover or forgiven debt.

Both banks' repayments precede new advances. Public alternating seat priority
resolves simultaneous requests; insufficient lender cash cancels an advance
without a fee. Acquisition finance settles earlier and can consume the same
cash. Every fill rechecks the actual remaining balance. Company cash
conservation and departmental payable reconciliations explicitly include the
new funding claims; they are not waived to accept the new state.

Public information: bank identity, aggregate lender cash, claims, equity,
standard terms and public settlement events. Private information: each player's
pending request, loan details and owner report. Lender transaction journals and
rival acquisition loan records are not copied into the public funding view.
Borrowing never buys shares or control. Future ownership records must reference
this same stable lender identity. Before outside-bank stake trading, define
issued shares, the current outside owner's investment basis, economic rights,
valuations, disclosure, dilution and funded transfers; reconcile them with
HoldingCapital and CompanyAuction rather than minting a second equity value.

## Player walkthrough

1. Start a new Expanded campaign. In Strategy → Research, inspect Commercial
   Banking and Digital Systems. Fund the five nodes in prerequisite order.
   Review the current capital limit as well as bank cash. Other research and
   projects compete for those resources.
2. In Banking → Business services → Corporate treasury → Set up service,
   compare the internal and partner routes. Add a reviewed deployment, then
   complete its funded execution work. No local digital premises are required.
3. Activate the completed platform. In Plan delivery capacity and People →
   Work coverage, reserve central Business time or contractors and adequate
   Technology support. Existing clients consume the same shared capacity.
4. Open View clients → Prospects. Inspect an eligible treasury mandate's need,
   price, costs, timing and capacity. Review the complete delivery proposal and
   stage a pursuit. Competitors or outside providers can still win it.
5. After a win, continue funding service and inspect next month's settled fees,
   collected cash, unpaid invoices and direct/contractor/platform costs.
   Retain shared staff and bank overhead in any profitability judgement.
6. Treasury → Outside-bank advance offers a separate reviewed one-month funding
   transaction. Inspect the next-month repayment before staging it; this is not
   equity capital and cannot fix a deficient capital ratio.
