## Permanent optional complexity — design decision

Meaningful optional systems are to remain configurable after they mature.
**Preview** describes maturity and acceptance limits, not a promise that a system
will later become compulsory. Related mechanics may remain one coherent module;
this does not require a switch for every internal rule. Accounting, determinism,
validation, save integrity, privacy/security and synchronization remain mandatory.
Complexity is separate from difficulty and never justifies hidden AI advantages.

Campaign setup must expose valid dependencies and the effective selected rules.
In multiplayer, the host selects one shared ruleset; changing it before play
resets readiness, and incompatible peers must not start. Campaign-start flags
are saved and generally fixed thereafter so disabling a module cannot erase
accounts, pending work, contracts or financial obligations. Resume/reconnect and
rematch preserve the rules unless a new campaign explicitly chooses otherwise.
Legacy saves keep their existing behavior; removing a Preview label does not
silently upgrade them.

The shared feature registry, dependency-confirmed selector and committed lobby
summary are now implemented in the current local batch; final release gates are
tracked separately. **Streamlined / Deeper / Custom** presets remain deferred
N-12 work. Existing opt-in flags and version checks remain authoritative, with a
largely cumulative prerequisite chain outside the bounded modular pilot. The target keeps
minimal genuine dependencies and mandatory foundations, not a permanent
requirement to enable every earlier preview. Presets must use only delivered,
supported modules, never imply access to unbuilt group or national content.
That future work must publish and test bounded
supported dependency profiles, module off/on boundaries, representative
interactions, invalid combinations, readiness reset, save continuity and peer
compatibility; it must not promise all 2^N combinations.

### Current bounded phase: remote onboarding pilot (v8.13 opt-in)

Begin with one selected market/segment/product channel, not a universal rewrite
of all acquisition. The current ordinary deposit and household flows settle
through separate paths; a universal queue needs a larger accounting redesign.

- Reserve a share of Retail sales time after retention and existing-customer
  offers. Processing and generating applications share that bounded budget;
  older eligible work goes first. Reserved channel time must replace the same
  ordinary acquisition effort, not add free production on top.
- Applications become eligible next month and expire after a short, explicit
  wait. They are nonbinding requests: no customer ownership, funding liability,
  cash, revenue or exclusive reservation exists while an application is pending.
- Activation jointly transfers available outside households and their segment
  deposits, consumes the same monthly quotas as ordinary intake and charges
  existing acquisition expense once. Clip to actual stock, staffing and affordable
  cost. Settle after existing-customer switching, repricing and credit repayment,
  before ordinary production; no immediate repeat switching or opening churn.
- Open the requested product at activation-month terms. Start savings promises
  then; cancel a closed/retired target instead of silently substituting one.
  Preserve the originating advertisement month and distinguish applications
  generated from accounts activated without claiming causal return on spending.
- Show pending, eligible, activated, expired and blocked work in Products with
  capacity/cost explanations. Gate a new opt-in version with bounded owner-private
  queues, strict save validation, deterministic partial fulfillment, empty-pool
  and contested-supply conservation, duplicate-cycle protection and all transports.
