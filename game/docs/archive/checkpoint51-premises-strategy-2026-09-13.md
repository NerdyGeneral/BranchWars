September 13, 2026. Scoped implementation evidence, not a full release or balance pass.

## Delivered

New Expanded source campaigns select save9.27 through the existing edition control. The five-benefit confirmation mentions shared service rooms; no extra checkbox or dependency list was added. Subsidiaries and rooms still require player decisions, funding and actual construction. Core and historical saves retain their rules.

The rival uses owner-visible demand, actual maintained permissions, finite qualified time and the same protected quotes as human orders. It preserves central capacity, avoids overbooking paid construction and only considers fit-out when recorded related earnings and both bank/subsidiary reserves support it. It does not use rival private books or create money, employees, clients or credentials.

Funded full-turn tests open a second real office, pay for two rooms, reserve two quarters from the same adviser, close one office through its normal project and replay identical orders. Closed locations cease local delivery while the remaining office survives. Separate tests release the actual adviser and verify no phantom local charges.
