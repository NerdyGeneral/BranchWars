# Checkpoint55 — paid research, adoption and service delivery

September 13, 2026. [Current implementation ledger](../v3-usability.md).

## Player outcome

Research's selected service application now shows existing signed agreements,
their signed fee total, and how many the prepared monthly plan can serve. Draft
fees are explicitly before collection risk and expenses, not realized receipts,
profit or customers granted by research. Invalid preparation shows an unavailable
estimate instead of inventing a number. The activation link opens the service
controls and focuses the selected platform when it is available.

No simulation, prices, research gates, AI, saved fields or campaign rules changed.
The existing in-house, licensed, partnered and purchased-service choices remain.
No requirement for four artificial routes per product was introduced.

## Whole Expanded acceptance

`tests/research_delivery_expanded.test.js` uses real paid research, submitted
monthly plans and ordinary opening resources. It does not grant milestones,
finish projects directly, assign contract ownership or inject customer balances.
Every resolved month checks game/ledger validity and exact save/recovery state.

- Rewards and promotional savings: both in-house and licensed launches, research
  prerequisites, actual shared-capacity completion, targeted funded deposits,
  pure forecasts, vendor costs and costed retirement with existing books retained.
- Treasury: Commercial research, paid partner launch, explicit activation,
  technology-supported finite service capacity, a real competitive mandate,
  continued servicing during Digital research and in-house replacement. The
  existing agreement survives; platform upkeep changes from $18K to $6K rather
  than stacking both bills.
- Payroll: Network and Operations research, paid automation, a real won mandate,
  $4K active upkeep and $1.5K direct servicing cost. Deactivation ends upkeep and
  restores $3K direct servicing cost without deleting the client.

The treasury acceptance initially failed because its prescribed staffing produced
only 2.67 effective points after Technology coverage, below the three-point
mandate. No bid was eligible. This was a test-plan error, not evidence that the
engine should waive capacity. The corrected plan uses the prepared quote and
explicitly purchases missing Technology support. Another correction deep-copies
the standing price policy before editing the draft; test preparation must not
mutate the campaign. Failed attempts are not counted as passes.
