#### Corporate-finance kernel — deliberately not active campaign content yet

`company-finance.js` adds six separately balanced operating-company books and
explicit outside purchaser/supplier and creditor books. Opening profiles follow
approved D12 (6F cash, 72F productive assets, 18F debt, 60F equity); the
**provisional external purchaser cash endowment is 240F per company**. It is
outside both player banks and the existing household/deposit denominator.
Subsequent sales are simultaneously prorated from actual outside cash; wages,
suppliers, service fees, interest, principal and dividends have paired receipts.
Unpaid supplier/interest bills remain claims with the correct counterparty.
Principal is not an expense and unpaid scheduled principal carries forward.
Outside shareholders receive bounded 30%-of-positive-profit distributions,
subject to retained earnings, obligations and 3F cash reserves.

The original kernel passed 21 checks / 1,920 months, but contraction accumulated
unbounded supplier claims. The current provisional wind-down rule ends trading
at exhausted equity or 33F unpaid bills (three normal 11F operating/service
months). Actual outside cash buys productive assets at at most half book value;
the remaining book value is an aggregate loss. Secured lender claims are paid
first, then suppliers, with actual residual capital returned to outside owners.
Unpaid claims are written off on BOTH books. Closed companies never restart or
accrue phantom sales, interest or expenses. Scarce buyer-cash priority rotates;
monthly sales finish before any asset purchase so proceeds cannot be reused in
another company's same-month sales. A first 3F threshold failed too readily in
the 480-month scenarios and was replaced with the three-operating-month measure.
These are documented provisional values, not established fun/balance acceptance.

The revised pure kernel passes 28 checks and 1,920 months across four 480-month
paths. Six companies survive neutral conditions, five sustained high demand,

#### D13 approved provisional contract — bounded shares and financed control

September 7 update: Option A is approved, including evidence-led tuning of its
numerical parameters. Auctions/control are not implemented yet. Existing

**Option A, recommended: monthly limit-order call auction.** This is a proposed
in-game market design. It excludes short selling, margin for ordinary share
trades, derivatives, circular ownership and trading the two player banks.

- Each parent can stage one buy **or** sell order per issuer per month. Orders
  specify whole shares and a limit price in cents, last for that resolution only,
  and reserve maximum cash plus fees or already owned unreserved shares. No
  reuse of unsettled sales proceeds. Unfilled reservations are released; repeated
  messages cannot repeat a fill. All trades have a real cash-paying buyer and
  share-owning seller.
- Each issuer has one explicit aggregate outside holder, initially owning its
  whole float under D12. Proposed outside trading cash starts at **25% of that
  issuer's initial equity**, outside the player groups. It may offer at most
  **2% of issued shares per side per month**, further capped by actual inventory
  and cash. No inventory or cash is refilled for free after trading.
- Outside quotes are **2% below/above** the last published reference price.
  Initially reference value is D12 equity per share. Subsequently use last settled
  net assets plus **six times the positive trailing-six-month average monthly
  operating profit**, divided by issued shares; nonpositive total reference value
  suspends outside buying instead of promising a floor. Publish inputs and age.
  The auction maximizes executable volume, then minimizes imbalance, then distance
  from the previous reference. Equal-price orders receive proportional integer
  fills under a documented deterministic tie rule, not permanent host priority.
- Proposed transaction fee: **0.25% of consideration per filled side**, charged
  once to an explicit outside exchange. No order submission/cancellation fee.
  Price movements revalue minority holdings separately from operating profit;
  realized proceeds, cost basis, fees and share/cash totals reconcile. Report
  thin liquidity and slippage, never an unlimited sale at the displayed mark.
- Proposed dividends: **30% of positive current net income**, additionally capped
  by retained earnings and actual cash after due debt/service obligations and a
  reserve of **three standard service fees (3 × F)**. Distributions reduce issuer
  cash/equity and pay every holder pro rata, including outside owners. They are
  not extra bank deposits or a second consolidated profit stream.
- A parent may commission diligence before an explicit control offer. Proposed
  fee: **1% of published issuer equity, minimum $5,000 / maximum $50,000**; one
  month of review reveals audited issuer obligations and sensitivities, not the
  rival's orders. Approval expires after three months or a material capital event.
- A control offer targets **more than 50% of issued votes**; an existing minority
  stake counts but never grants free contracts. Outside shares will tender only
  at **at least 15% above** the last reference and only if the offer can actually
  reach control. Shares already owned by the rival require its explicit consent.
  Competing eligible offers choose highest price, then greater committed equity
  funding, then a published deterministic rotating tie rule. Losers release their
  purchase reservation but do not recover consumed diligence costs.
- Acquisition-only outside financing may fund up to **40% of purchase cash**,
  with **0.75% monthly interest / 36-month straight-line principal**. It is parent
  debt, not an automatic loan from its bank or the target. Require positive
  trailing earnings and **1.5× coverage of projected debt service** by the buyer's
  existing distributable cash earnings plus the purchased share of target earnings.
  Actual cash equity, fees and reserves must be present. D09 governs support;
  borrowing never bypasses segregated customer assets or protected bank reserves.
- Review takes one month; failure to fund or a failed eligibility recheck cancels
  the purchase without transferring shares. A successful acquisition pays sellers,
  records existing liabilities and purchase premium, and enters **six integration
  work units**, with **one executive execution-capacity unit** occupied while
  active and **1% of purchase cash in total integration expense**, spread over
  the completed work. No instant merger dividend or automatic customer capture.
- A controlled target can reject a rival offer or pay **1% of its published
  equity** for an independently funded review defense delaying that offer by one
  month, once per offer. No free share dilution or cancellation of existing votes.
  Understaffing/integration disruption affects service delivery through explicit
  capacity, not magical deposit confiscation. Pausing integration preserves
  liabilities, remaining work and costs; the buyer cannot reset a deal for free.
