# V2 — Connected Company Banking

## Included

- Existing optional complexity, dependency confirmations, editable multiplayer
  lobbies, bank colors and fixed campaign rules are retained.
- Deposit pricing/programmes, advertising, relationships, onboarding, workforce,
  collections, loans and the existing regional banking systems remain available.
- Financial Group parent/bank accounting and guarded capital transfers; a shared
  origination budget across mortgage, middle-market and consumer lending.
- Six operating corporate clients with finite cash, sales, expenses, debt and
  liquidation. Their signed banking mandates are independent of ownership.
- Company-paid bank fees, unpaid receivables, later collection and real write-offs.
  Receivables are not spendable cash and carry capital risk. Defaults close
  mandates rather than award them to the rival for free.
- Group company statements, private bank receipts, forecast and causal-ledger
  integration; older peers cannot silently downgrade a new-rules campaign.

## Known limitations

- AI lending remains strongly mortgage-biased. Some stress runs use alternatives,
  but balanced samples do not demonstrate broad strategy diversity.
- All six companies can close in long stress campaigns. There is no automatic
  replacement/restart or completed company acquisition system in this milestone.
- Deposit dominance is possible and is not by itself an exploit. No artificial
  catch-up or equal-outcome tuning was added for release.
- Forecasts use public company contracts and assume rival delivery; concealed
  rival staffing/orders, future economics and random events can change results.
- Company accounting reconciles the new fee boundary, not every legacy income
  stream against an individually modeled company.
- Human fun, comeback viability and physical two-computer GitHub/LAN reliability
  are not certified by automated tests.
- A separate unfinished agency experiment is preserved locally, not included.
