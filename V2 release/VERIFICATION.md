## Two-computer acceptance checklist

1. Both computers open this same V2 build; host exports an existing game first.
2. Create/join a room, choose distinguishable colors, apply desired previews,
   confirm that both players see the same settings and readiness.
3. Submit several simultaneous turns; check that both views agree on the cycle,
   public totals and company contracts while private plans stay concealed.
4. Briefly disconnect/reconnect one player; use Resume/Retry and verify there is
   no duplicated turn, charge or lost committed plan.
5. Export, reopen and Continue/import. Verify colors, campaign options, company
   receivables and saved progress persist.
6. Play a longer competitive session and report meaningful choices, dead periods,
   comeback opportunities and any confusing or cramped screen.
